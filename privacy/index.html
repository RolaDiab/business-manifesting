4. Cookies and Tracking

We use two categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary: required for the site to function, such as Stripe's fraud-prevention cookies on its hosted checkout page. These do not require consent and cannot be turned off.
  • Analytics and advertising: used to understand site traffic and measure our marketing campaigns (for example, Google Analytics or Meta/Google ad pixels). Where a site uses them, they only load after you accept them on that site's cookie banner. They are off by default. businessmanifesting.com does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

On sites that show a cookie banner, you can change your choice at any time via "Cookie Settings" in the site footer.

Do Not Track. There is no common standard for browser "Do Not Track" signals, so our sites do not currently respond to them. Instead, analytics and advertising cookies stay off unless you accept them.

Tracking by other companies. Where you accept analytics or advertising cookies, Google and Meta may collect information about your visits to our sites and to other websites over time. Where you do not accept them, they do not load.

5. Third-Party Services

We share personal data with the following third-party services, which process it on our behalf or as independent controllers, strictly to provide the site and services described above:

  • Adhara: our CRM, forms, and event-management platform. Adhara processes the information you submit through our forms (registrations, waitlist signups, contact requests, assessment sign-ups), manages our event listings, and is used to send you the emails you've opted into. Adhara acts as a data processor on our behalf.
  • GoHighLevel: our CRM and email platform. It stores your contact details and purchase history, hosts our checkout pages, and sends the emails you've requested or agreed to receive. GoHighLevel acts as a data processor on our behalf.
  • ManyChat: the platform that runs our Instagram direct messages. If you message us on Instagram, ManyChat processes your Instagram username, your messages, the options you tap, and any email address you share, and passes your email and choices to GoHighLevel.
  • Stripe: for secure payment processing.
  • Supabase: our backend infrastructure provider, used to run our forms and website functions, connect to our payment processor, and manage the delivery of transactional emails (such as receipts and account notices).
  • Email delivery infrastructure: used to send transactional and marketing emails you've requested, and to process unsubscribe requests and bounce/complaint handling.
  • Website hosting: our sites are hosted by GitHub Pages, Vercel and Lovable. Like any web host, they receive your IP address and basic browser information when you visit, and may keep it in short-term server logs.
  • Google Fonts: loads the typefaces on our sites. Your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Zoom: used to host the live webinars and trainings you register for. When you join a live session, Zoom processes data under its own privacy policy.

These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle your data.

6. Marketing Consent and Unsubscribing

Where we ask for your consent to send marketing emails, the checkbox is unticked by default, and in Instagram direct messages consent is a separate button from the free guide. You must actively opt in. We do not send marketing emails to non-customers without it. If you buy from us, we tell you at checkout that we will also send occasional emails about our related products and programs, and you can opt out at any time with one click in any email (the "existing customer" basis allowed in the UK, EU and similar laws). You can withdraw your consent at any time, free of charge, by using the unsubscribe link included in every marketing email or by emailing rola@alorsolutions.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before you withdrew it, and does not affect transactional emails (such as receipts or event access details) tied to a product or event you've registered for.

Where we transfer personal data from the EEA or UK to a country that has not been deemed to provide an adequate level of data protection, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, or on the third-party service's own compliance mechanisms.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with similar data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data, including for direct marketing.
  • Request data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email rola@alorsolutions.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. Children

Our sites and products are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email rola@alorsolutions.com and we will delete it.

10. Automated Decisions

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Our free assessment calculates a score from your answers to personalize your results and the emails you receive. It does not decide whether you can buy or join anything.

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law (for example, tax and accounting records). If you unsubscribe from marketing emails, we retain a minimal suppression record (your email address) to ensure we don't contact you again. You may request deletion of your data at any time, subject to our legal retention obligations.
