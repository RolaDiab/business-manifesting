Weekly on Mondays for 12 weeks, starting February 8, 2027. Each call is 90 minutes on Zoom. The exact time is shared before kickoff, and every call is recorded.

I haven't started my business yet. Is this too early?

No. If you have a clear idea and you're ready to act on it during the 12 weeks, this program is built for you.

Plan for about 3 hours: the 90-minute call, a short guided practice, and one real-life assignment.

Both. The performance side draws on neuroscience and behavioral psychology. The energy side is clearing work you feel in your body. Each makes the other work better.
