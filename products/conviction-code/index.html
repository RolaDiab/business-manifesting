Why it works

Built on research, not hype.

Conviction is the multiplier on real skill, not a replacement for it. Every tool in the program rests on work like this.

Specific goals win

Specific, hard goals lead to better performance than “do your best.”

Locke & Latham, 2002

If-then plans hold

Decades of studies show that deciding “when X happens, I will do Y” raises follow-through.

Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006

Missing a day is fine

New habits took a median of 66 days to stick, and one missed day didn't derail them.

Lally et al., 2010

Nerves can be fuel

Saying “I am excited” before speaking beat trying to calm down.

Brooks, 2014
