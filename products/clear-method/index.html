Questions

FAQ

What do I need?

Your phone, tablet or computer, a quiet space, and headphones if you have them. Keep the workbook for after the Clearing.

How long does it take?

The Clearing itself runs about 10 minutes. With the short lessons and your writing time after, plan for about 30 minutes the first time.

Do I write during the Clearing?

No. The Clearing is eyes closed, start to finish, so nothing interrupts what's happening in your body. You write in the workbook afterwards.

What if the belief comes back?

Some beliefs clear in one round. Deeper ones can take two or three. Your Day 1, 7 and 30 check-ins show you when to run it again.

Can I use it more than once?

Yes. Run it on any belief, as often as you need.
