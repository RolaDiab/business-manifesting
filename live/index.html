Before you join

Is it really free?

Yes. It's a gift to the community.

How do I get the Zoom link?

By email. Before each session you'll receive an invitation with the date, the time in your time zone, and your link.

Will there be a replay?

No. Group Manifesting Live happens live only, so be there live. Sessions are recorded for the archive and are not shared.

Do I need my camera on?

Yes. Camera on is part of the experience, because the connection in your group depends on it. Participants who are off camera will be removed so your group stays whole.

What happens after?

You'll receive a short follow-up note. You're welcome to explore more, including the upcoming Business Manifesting Summit and the programs.

Can I work one-to-one?

Private one-to-one coaching is open to a select few each year. Apply here. You can also join the waitlist for group coaching.
